Read & explore

Swipe between feeds

Swipe left or right to discover different Nostr kinds: posts, highlights, photos, videos, articles, and replies. The icon pill shows your current feed and hints at its neighbors. The same swipe gesture switches profile tabs.

Profiles with more to discover

Browse dedicated tabs for someone’s posts, replies, media, reactions, bookmarks, people, projects, apps, issues, emoji packs, and zap activity. Tabs appear when there is content to show.

Articles made for reading

Scan compact article cards with author avatars, publication dates, and estimated reading time. Open the full article, then reply, react, or zap without leaving it.

Highlights in context

Read highlighted passages with a simple yellow treatment. Highlights have their own feed and profile tab, so the lines people save are easy to find.

Connected conversation threads

Open any note to see earlier notes above it and grouped reply branches below, with lines connecting the avatars. Your reading position stays put as context loads. Tap the vertical dots to reveal more replies in place. Quoted notes and profile mentions remain tappable, and cached notes appear immediately when available.

Notifications & profile search

See replies, reactions, and zaps directed at your account. Find public profiles through Vertex search, with a deliberately small search interface.

Photos, video & links

Full-screen media gallery

Swipe through photos with controls that get out of the way. Jump to the original note, react, or zap from the viewer. Profile pictures and banners form a two-image gallery.

VLC-style video controls

Videos autoplay muted, one at a time. Double-tap to seek in the feed, or open full screen for VLC-style gestures: swipe to seek, adjust brightness, and change volume. Download a video, share it, or copy its URL.

Media ready before you reach it

Images and nearby video data preload as you browse. Local caches and avatar thumbnails reduce repeat downloads; settings show storage usage and let you choose the budget.

GIF search & rich links

The composer’s GIF keyboard searches words and emoji with animated previews and autocomplete. Pick a GIF for your post or reply, powered by nostr.build. GIFs animate inline. Web links show previews when metadata is available, and YouTube cards include thumbnails. Open videos in a compatible installed app, including NewPipe.

Post, reply & appreciate

Posts & quick replies

Write a new post from the feather button or reply from a note. Tag people using profile search, see follow and identity indicators, and keep unfinished drafts on your device.

Images on your Blossom servers

Attach images with Android’s photo picker. Feather uploads to the Blossom servers listed on your profile and adds successful image URLs to your draft.

Reactions with personality

Tap for an orange heart, or hold for a sensible picker built around your recent and frequent reactions. Search the full emoji picker when you want something else, explore custom emoji packs, and tap your existing reaction to request its removal.

One-tap Lightning zaps

Connect a wallet through Nostr Wallet Connect (NWC), using a connection string or QR code. One tap sends 21 sats; hold to choose a recent amount or enter your own. Sensible presets appear when there is no history. Public activity includes zaps and nutzaps.

Native, with sane defaults

Your signer holds your keys

Sign in with an Android signer such as Amber. Feather asks it to authorize your actions; there is no private-key import or account-creation flow in the app.

Automatic relay discovery

Sane defaults, minimal configuration. Feather uses public relay lists to find profiles’ write relays and load their content. Discovery happens in the background, with no relay setup screen. Media loads automatically and the cache starts at 1 GB.

System light & dark mode

Native Android components, restrained blue accents, and an interface that follows your system theme. Icon controls leave the space to the content.

Content controls built in

Muted and blocked accounts are filtered out. Self-marked sensitive content is hidden by default, with a setting to show it when you choose.

NGIT · GRASP · NIP-34

Report bugs. Suggest features.
On Nostr.

Help build Feather with the Nostr identity you already use. Report a bug or suggest a feature directly in the app, and follow the discussion on GitWorkshop. No GitHub account needed.

Report from the app

Tap the bug icon in settings. After a crash, open a prefilled report with the stack trace, review or edit it, and send. Common secrets and URLs are removed from the prefilled details; nothing is sent automatically.

Your ideas, your identity

The feature-request icon opens a simple issue composer. Your Android signer signs the public report as a Nostr issue. Saved drafts and retries that reuse the same signed event keep an interrupted submission from becoming a duplicate.

Code mirrored with ngit

Feather’s source and release tags are mirrored on two GRASP servers alongside GitHub. ngit publishes signed repository state, while NIP-34 keeps bug reports and feature requests on Nostr.

Code & issues on GitWorkshop ↗Clone & contribute ↗

Explore public issues in Feather’s Issues tab, too. Reports are public under the identity that signs them.

About ngit & GRASP

ngit connects Git workflows to Nostr. GRASP servers host Git repositories and the Nostr relays used for collaboration. Feather uses relay.ngit.dev and gitnostr.com. GitHub remains a source mirror and the home of the downloadable APK releases.
