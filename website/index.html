featherA little space for your people.
01

Read at your pace.

Notes from the people you follow, newest first. Photos load automatically. Videos play silently as you scroll. Recent notes and media stay cached on your device.

02

Keep your keys.

Connect an Android signer like Amber to read your following feed. Your keys stay in the signer. Feather never publishes.

03

Leave a little out.

No posting, search, DMs, ads, or analytics. Just reading. Relay connections pause when you leave.

Less app.
More breathing room.
