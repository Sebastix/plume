feather

Privacy

Feather has no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

On your device

The app stores your public account key, signer app name, relay settings, draft, and a bounded cache of public events. Feather does not import or store your private Nostr key. Signing is handled by the Android signer you choose. Android backup is disabled for app data.

On Nostr

The relays you connect to receive your IP address and the requests needed to load notes, profiles, and follows. Notes and follow lists you publish are public. Other people and relays may retain copies. Relay connections pause when Feather is backgrounded.

External links

Links open in your browser or another installed app. Those services have their own privacy policies. The initial release displays text notes and does not automatically load remote images.

This website

This static website is hosted on Vercel. It does not add cookies or analytics. Vercel processes requests and may retain infrastructure logs under its own privacy policy.

Questions

See the source code or open an issue. Please do not include private keys or other secrets.
